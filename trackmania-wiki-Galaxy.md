# TrackMania² Galaxy

'''TrackMania² Galaxy''' is a free [[title pack]] for [[ManiaPlanet]] created by '''Dommy''' (domino54). It is a multi-environment title pack featuring multiple game modes, with the flagship mode being '''Pursuit''' (cops vs thieves). Originally released as '''TrackMania² Pursuit''' in 2014, the title was officially renamed to '''TrackMania² Galaxy''' in 2019 (Title Update 1.6).

The title pack is available for free — no Planets payment required.

== Overview ==

TrackMania² Galaxy is an ever-expanding collection of various game modes introducing new gameplay mechanics and tactics to learn. Run for your freedom as a thief or execute the law as a cop in an intensive Pursuit match. Discover a new way of racing by hunting checkpoints in Goal Hunt. Hunt your opponents and avoid being hunted in Hunters.

The title pack was designed as a unified collection of game modes, inspired by Dommy's abandoned [[ShootMania Galaxy]] title. Galaxy is meant to be a shared ecosystem with a shared profile, stats, achievements, and unlockables across both TrackMania and ShootMania.

== Game Modes ==

=== Pursuit ===
{{Main|Pursuit (Galaxy)}}

The flagship mode. Players are divided into two teams: '''Police''' (who chase and capture thieves) and '''Thieves''' (who survive and score points by evading capture). Pursuit supports multiple environments including Canyon, Stadium, Valley, and Lagoon.

=== Goal Hunt ===

A checkpoint hunting race mode where each player has an individual goal checkpoint. Reach your goal to score points and receive a new random goal. Combines racing with strategic checkpoint selection.

=== Hunters ===

A free-for-all hunt mode: catch opponents to score points and avoid getting caught yourself. Everyone is both hunter and prey.

== Title Update History ==

=== Title Update 1.2 — "Enhanced Points System" (January 2018) ===

This update introduced a completely overhauled, dynamic scoring system designed to balance the matches between the Police and the Thieves.

* '''Capture Points Scaling''' — Early-game captures are worth 1 point; late-game captures scale up to 5 points per capture as fewer thieves remain.
* '''Survival Points''' — Every active thief receives 1 survival point whenever another thief is caught.
* '''Last Thief Bonus''' — Securing the final surviving thief awards up to 5 bonus points.

=== Title Update 1.3 — "Construction Update" (June 2018) ===

* '''Vehicle Selection Screen''' — Shows a picture of each car, pros and cons, and percentage-based stats.
* '''Map-Specific Gameplay Options''' — Mappers can choose vehicles, force different cars for Police and Thieves, choose gameplay settings, and lock minimap rotation.
* '''New OffZone Types''' — Danger Zone (8-second escape timer) and Police Zone (police-only routes).

=== Title Update 1.3.1 (July 2018) ===

* '''Zone Items by florenzius''' — Decorative indicators for all three zone types: yellow (Danger Zone), red (OffZone), blue (Police Zone).
* These items are purely decorative — mappers must place zones manually using the Pursuit OffZone plugin.

=== Title Update 1.4 — "4 Years of Pursuing" (February 2019) ===

Released on the 4th anniversary of the mode's first release.

* '''Capture Assists''' — Detects who contributed to catching a thief besides the capturer. Assisting gives 1 point.
* '''TrackMania Forever Vehicles''' — All 6 "new" TMF vehicles available without Planet paywalls.
* '''Goal Hunt Pursuit''' — A variant combining Pursuit with Goal Hunt mechanics.
* '''Speedbomb''' — A mechanic that ignites a bomb when driving at slow speed (5 seconds of inactivity triggers explosion).
* '''3 New Zones''' — Thief Zone (thieves-only), Speedbomb Zone (200 km/h minimum), Police Danger Zone (combo zone).
* '''Fuel & Charging Stations''' — Cars have a gas tank; refill at Charging Station objects.
* '''Race Objectives''' — Points on tall/climbing-heavy maps; first to reach gets the most points.
* '''Quality of Life''' — Thieves buffed (up to 4 survival points), Lagoon Car changes, spawn delay, no spawn multi-kills.

=== Title Update 1.6 — "Galaxy" (2019) ===

The title pack was officially renamed from TrackMania² Pursuit to TrackMania² Galaxy. The Pursuit mode name remains unchanged.

> "It doesn't mean the Pursuit mode changes its name, it's just a rename of the title pack. The reason behind this change is my will to continue the legacy of my abandoned ShootMania Galaxy title."
> — Dommy (domino54)

Both the multi-environment and Stadium variants were renamed. The title is now free of Planets payment.

A '''Canary''' development pack was also released for testing new features before they reach the main title.
